# Responsible AI Principles

NeuroBridge AI is intended to support people, not replace clinicians, therapists, caregivers, teachers, or families.

## Principles

- Use synthetic or properly consented data during development.
- Minimize data collection and retain only what is necessary.
- Keep recommendations explainable and reviewable by humans.
- Avoid diagnostic claims unless a future system is clinically validated and appropriately regulated.
- Evaluate bias across age, communication style, support needs, and other relevant populations.
- Provide clear uncertainty and confidence information when AI models are introduced.
- Keep an audit trail of recommendations and user overrides.
- Design for role-based access and strong privacy controls.

## Current phase

The current repository uses synthetic examples only. No protected health information or personally identifiable therapy data is required to run the starter prototype.
